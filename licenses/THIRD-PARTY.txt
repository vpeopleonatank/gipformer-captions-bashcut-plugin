bin/provider links these libraries statically (from the sherpa-onnx v1.13.8 osx-arm64 static-lib release):

- sherpa-onnx, kaldi-native-fbank, kaldi-decoder, kaldifst, OpenFst, ssentencepiece: Apache-2.0 (sherpa-onnx-LICENSE.txt)
- ONNX Runtime: MIT (onnxruntime-LICENSE.txt)
- kissfft: BSD-3-Clause
- piper-phonemize: MIT
- espeak-ng: GPL-3.0-or-later (https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng). It comes in with sherpa-onnx's text-to-speech
  code, which this plugin never calls; the speech recognizer does not use it.
- ucd (Unicode character data): see https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
